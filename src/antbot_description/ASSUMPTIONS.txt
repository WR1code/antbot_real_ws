GK4XC-001-003 CAD model conventions
===================================
Coordinate convention: +X front, +Y left, +Z up.
The source SolidWorks STEP uses millimetres and is rotated -90 degrees around
ROS Y in base.xacro. The resulting visual envelope is approximately
0.551 x 0.819 x 1.286 m (ROS X x Y x Z).
The full CAD assembly is the only vehicle visual. Collision geometry remains
simplified and all eight steering/drive joint names remain compatible.
Sensor TF frames are retained separately because STEP does not encode ROS TF.
